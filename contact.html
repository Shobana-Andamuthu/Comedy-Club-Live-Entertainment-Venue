ARRIVAL GUIDE

Getting to The Velvet Mic

Located in the vibrant center of the Downtown Theater Arts District, our venue is easily accessible by metro transit, valet parking, and rideshare services.

Valet & Garage Parking

Valet is available at our front entrance for $15 starting at 6:00 PM. Covered self-park garage is located 1 block east on 4th & Speakeasy.

Public Transit Access

Red & Purple Subway Lines stop at Theater District Station, just a 3-minute stroll to our marquee doors.

Rideshare Drop-off Zone

Designated Uber/Lyft passenger loading zone directly in front of our brightly lit marquee archway.

LIVE BOX OFFICE HOURS

Box Office Open 6 Nights a Week

Doors open at 5:00 PM Tuesday through Sunday. Show up early for happy hour craft cocktails at our subterranean bar before showtime.
