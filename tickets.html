FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Ticketing & House Guidelines

Key details to help prepare for an unforgettable night of live stand-up comedy.

Like premier comedy venues worldwide, all ticketed admissions require a two-item food or beverage minimum purchase per guest inside the showroom. We offer an extensive selection of signature craft cocktails, premium wine, zero-proof mocktails, and artisanal shareable bites.

Unless specifically noted as an All-Ages Family Matinee, all regular evening shows are strictly 21+ with valid government-issued photo ID required at the door. No exceptions are granted for underage patrons during adult headline shows.

Doors open 60 minutes prior to the scheduled showtime. For General Admission ticket holders, we recommend arriving at least 30-45 minutes before curtain to secure your preferred seats and place your initial drink order before lights dim.

Tickets are non-refundable once purchased. However, if you notify our box office at least 24 hours prior to the show, we can transfer your credit toward any future performance within 60 days of the original date.
