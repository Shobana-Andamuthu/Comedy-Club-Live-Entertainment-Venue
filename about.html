THE BEGINNING

Founded on Pure Comedy Craft

Established in 2008 in the historic Theater Arts District, The Velvet Mic was born out of a desire for an authentic comic's room. A stage stripped of gimmickry, where writers could test raw material and audiences could witness comedic history in an intimate, electric room.

Over nearly two decades, our stage has hosted surprise drop-ins from Grammy-winning comedians, world-renowned television hosts, and groundbreaking rising voices shaping contemporary culture.

COMEDY WORKSHOP

The Writers Lab & New Voice Incubator

Beyond evening ticketed performances, The Velvet Mic operates a weekday writers lab. Emerging comedians receive mentorship, premise punch-up workshops, and audio recording resources to sharpen their touring sets.
